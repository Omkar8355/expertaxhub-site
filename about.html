Expert Tax Hub LLP is a Chembur-based advisory firm, built around one idea: taxation, legal compliance and investment planning work better under one roof.

Our Story

Started in 2008, by a founder who believed in staying close to clients.

CA Sunil Jain founded Expert Tax Hub in Chembur, Mumbai in late 2008, as an income tax and compliance practice. Over the years, the firm's scope grew to match what clients actually needed — legal representation when a notice arrived, and investment guidance that didn't ignore the tax consequences.

Today, Expert Tax Hub works with individuals, businesses and cooperative housing societies across Mumbai — on everything from a routine GST return to a scrutiny notice that needs a carefully worded reply.
