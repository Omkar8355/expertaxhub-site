Questions

Legal Advisory FAQs

Can you reply to a legal notice on our society's behalf?

Yes — we've drafted replies for cooperative housing societies, including GST scrutiny matters and ITC reversal defences.

Do you draft agreements and sale deeds?

Yes, including converting existing legal documents like sale deeds and confirmation letters into properly formatted documents.

What do you need from us to draft a notice reply?

The exact notice, and the specific points you want covered. If you give us exact points, we draft to those — we don't add extra grounds beyond what's specified.
