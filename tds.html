Questions

TDS FAQs

Do I need to deduct TDS on contractor payments?

Generally yes, above specified thresholds under Section 194C/194J depending on the nature of payment. We can confirm the exact rate for your case.

What is PTEC and does my business need it?

Professional Tax Enrollment Certificate — required for business owners and professionals in Maharashtra. We've handled PTEC registers across multiple financial years for clients.

I got a TRACES default notice for short deduction — what now?

This is fixable through a correction statement. Send us the notice and we'll identify the gap and file the correction.
